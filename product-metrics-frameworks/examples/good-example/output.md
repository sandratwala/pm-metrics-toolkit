## North Star Metric Framework

**North Star Metric:** Monthly referred users successfully onboarded (completed first paid task via a referral link)

**Input/driver metrics:**
- Referral link share rate — % of active users who generate a referral link
- Referral click-through rate — link clicks ÷ shares
- Referral signup conversion rate — signups ÷ clicks
- Reward redemption rate — % of eligible referral rewards claimed
- Time-to-first-referral — days from activation to a user sending their first invite

**Rationale:** Epic 1 (invite flow) and Epic 2 (rewards) both center on getting a referred user to a completed paid action, and the JTBD "I want to easily invite friends... and get rewarded" ties the referrer's action directly to a converted referee — making completed referrals the clearest single measure of value delivered.

## Pirate Metrics Framework (AARRR)

**Acquisition:** New signups attributed to a referral link, per month
**Activation:** % of referred signups completing their first task within 7 days of signup
**Retention:** 30-day retention rate of referred users vs. non-referred users (cohort comparison)
**Referral:** Referral participation rate (% of active users generating ≥1 link/month); referral K-factor (viral coefficient)
**Revenue:** Premium conversion rate among referred users; free-month reward redemptions that convert to paid subscriptions

*(HEART was not applied here — the PRD's epics and JTBD are framed entirely around inviting, converting, and rewarding referred users, which are growth/funnel concerns; there's no stated usability or task-completion goal distinct from the referral funnel itself.)*

## Business Metrics

- **CAC reduction from referral channel** — target 15–20% reduction in paid acquisition spend (stated directly in Impact)
- **Referral-driven revenue share** — % of MRR originating from referred-user premium conversions (ties Solution's reward-to-conversion mechanism to revenue)
- **Cost per referred acquisition vs. paid CAC** — efficiency comparison grounded in the stated CAC reduction claim
- **90-day retention lift, referred vs. paid-channel users** — explicitly called out in Impact as a trust-based onboarding effect
- **Reward payout cost ratio** — total reward cost ÷ revenue generated by referred users, grounded in the Solution's automatic reward-crediting mechanism
