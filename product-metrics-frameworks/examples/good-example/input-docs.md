# Input: PRD (excerpt)

**Product:** TaskFlow (task management app for freelancers)
**Feature:** Social Referral Program

**Epics:**
- Epic 1: Referral invite flow — users generate a unique referral link and send it via email or direct share.
- Epic 2: Referral rewards — referrer and referee each receive one free month of Premium when the referee's first paid task is completed.
- Epic 3: Referral leaderboard & social sharing — in-app leaderboard showing top referrers, shareable to social platforms.

**Jobs To Be Done:**
- "When I love using TaskFlow, I want to easily invite my freelancer friends so they can benefit too and I get rewarded."
- "When I'm evaluating a new tool, I want to trust a friend's referral more than an ad."
- "When I invite people, I want to track how many joined and get credit automatically, without manual follow-up."

---

# Input: Research Report (excerpt)

**Solution:** Build a lightweight referral flow embedded in the dashboard: unique referral links, automatic reward crediting on the referee's first paid action, and a leaderboard to gamify sharing.

**Impact:** Referral-driven signups are projected to reduce paid acquisition spend by 15–20%. Because referred users onboard through a trusted recommendation rather than an ad, we expect a measurable lift in 90-day retention versus paid-channel users. Reward-driven premium unlocks are also expected to accelerate free-to-paid conversion among referred users.
